The control chain

How a controlled system is built

  1. PlantThe physical system: its mass, inertia, thermal capacity, actuators and sensors.
  2. ModelGoverning ODEs, transfer function or state-space form, with stated assumptions.
  3. SimulationOpen-loop response: step, impulse and frequency behaviour.
  4. ControllerPID or state feedback tuned against stability and performance targets.
  5. Controlled responseClosed-loop results, disturbance rejection and, where possible, hardware validation.
