Core CAD Disciplines

Parametric Modelling

3D solid modelling built on driving dimensions and design intent, with configurations and design tables so a part family updates from one model.

Assemblies

Mate-driven assemblies, weldments and motion studies — checked for interference and access before a single part gets machined.

Surfacing

Complex, non-prismatic geometry — lofts, sweeps and surface patches for forms that solid features alone can't produce cleanly.

Engineering Drawings

Production-ready drawings with full GD&T, tolerancing and revision control — the document that actually goes to the shop floor.
