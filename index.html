Technical workflow

From physical system to validated controller

  1. Physical SystemGeometry, materials, loads and operating conditions.
  2. Mathematical ModelGoverning equations, assumptions, boundary and initial conditions.
  3. Numerical SimulationMesh, solver and physics set-up in ANSYS, MATLAB or Python.
  4. Engineering AnalysisPlots and contours interpreted physically, not just reported.
  5. Control / OptimizationControllers and parameter studies built on the model.
  6. ValidationChecks against theory, hand calculations or measurements.
